# Hoop Roll

Play: https://hooproll.rennko.tech

Basketball card-pack sim with a retro trading-card look: wax packs, numbered parallels with serials, card backs with ratings, a binder, play-by-play games and a newspaper front page at the end of each season. Each season you get 30 rolls to open packs (Standard, Deluxe, Elite) with 2K-style tiers Bronze→Galaxy, holo variants, pity, walkout animations and a collection book, building a 10-man roster of real NBA players, then sim a 24-game season vs 9 AI teams and a top-4 playoff. Titles and best record persist in localStorage. AI teams get tougher each season.

Source: `src/game.html` (page + logic) and `src/players.js` (player list); `./build.sh` inlines them into `dist/index.html`. Ratings are rough fan estimates.

    ./build.sh          # copies to dist/ and opens it
    ./build.sh deploy   # publishes to https://hooproll.rennko.tech


## Player photos
`art.js` is generated by `node tools/fetch_art.mjs`: it asks the Wikipedia API for each player's free-licence lead photo (Wikimedia Commons) and records author + licence, shown under "Photo credits" in the game. `tools/titles.json` overrides ambiguous article titles. Players without a free photo fall back to an emoji silhouette.

## Admin panel
Type `admin` anywhere (not in a text box) to open it: extra rolls/coins, force Galaxy/Holo pulls, give any player, fill roster, sim to playoffs, unlock collection, reset save.

Player photos: Wikimedia Commons contributors (credited in-game). Ratings are fan estimates; not affiliated with the NBA.
